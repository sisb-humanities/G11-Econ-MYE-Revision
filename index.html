Paper 1 quick-fire

Twelve multiple-choice questions per round, drawn from 33. Paper 1 is 30 questions in 1 hour, so aim for 2 minutes each. Read all four options before you choose.

On paper: the Paper 1 tips in Revision Guide Section 7. Write down the topic of every question you get wrong and check it in Section 2.

4

Shifts and diagrams

Fourteen events: ten markets and four PPCs. Decide what moves, which way, what happens to price and quantity, and what happens to consumer or producer surplus. The diagram draws itself when you finish.

On paper: Revision Guide Section 3 (Diagram workshop): model diagrams and drawing drills D1–D8.

5

Section A skills

Question 1 is point-marked: each correct, explained point earns a mark, up to a limit for each part. Practise three skills: the 2-mark definition, the 4-mark ‘explain and consider’ and the 6-mark ‘assess’.

On paper: then do the data-response question in Revision Guide Section 8 (Bangkok’s 20-baht train fare). Parts (a)(i), (b) and (c) use these three skills.

6

Section B builder

Build each essay: tap a sentence to place it in the next empty slot; tap a filled slot to send it back. The labels show which part of the mark scheme each sentence earns. One sentence in every set does not belong, so leave it out. Then read the model and write the very similar question beside it, on paper, in your Revision Guide.

On paper: Revision Guide Section 9. Remember the rule that decides the most marks: a one-sided answer gets no evaluation marks in a 12-mark essay.
